VANS – OFF THE WALL

SINCE 1966

THE STORY OF

VANS

1966

Vans is founded in California. First shoe: Authentic.

1970s

Skateboarders adopt Vans. Brand grows in skate culture.

1976–1978

Era, Old Skool, Slip-On, and Sk8-Hi are introduced.

1982

Slip-Ons become famous in pop culture.

1984

Company files for bankruptcy but later recovers.

1995

Launch of Warped Tour and expansion into events.

2000s

Global growth, collaborations, and custom shoes.

2016

Vans celebrates 50 years.

2017+

New designs like UltraRange and global competitions.
