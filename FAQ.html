Shipping

What are your shipping policies and delivery timeframes?

Processing:

  • Before 11am (Mon–Fri): same-day processing
  • After 11am: next business day
  • Friday after 11am: processed Monday
  • Weekends/Holidays: no processing

Shipping Times:

  • Standard: 3–6 business days
  • Two-Day: 2–3 business days
  • Overnight: 1–2 business days
  • Priority Mail: 5–7 business days

Custom Products: 3–5 weeks

Items may ship separately. No weekend delivery (except USPS).

How much does shipping cost?

Standard Shipping

  • Members: Free $50+, otherwise $5
  • Guests: Free $99+, otherwise $8

Express Shipping (starting):

  • Shoes: $21.95
  • Accessories/Clothing: $18.95
  • Backpacks: $18.95
Do you ship internationally?

Currently, shipping is only available within the U.S. Use regional sites for international orders.

Do you ship to P.O. Boxes or military addresses?

Yes, via USPS only. Express shipping and custom shoes are not available.

When will I be charged for pickup orders?

What days do you offer delivery?

No. If an item is out of stock, you won’t be charged. You’ll be notified of updates.

You’ll see a pre-authorization hold and the final charge. The hold disappears after a few days.

Returns & Exchanges

Does Vans offer exchanges?

Currently, Vans does not offer online exchanges, but please contact Customer Support for assistance. You may exchange an online order in any Vans Store or Vans Outlet. If exchanging in-store, we recommend calling the store first to confirm availability.

What is your return policy?

Online Vans.com orders can be returned within 30 days of purchase.

Custom products are not eligible for return. Purchases at Vans retail locations must be returned in-store.

Certain items are final sale and cannot be returned.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method, excluding original shipping charges.

Extended Holiday Return Policy: Orders placed after 11/1/2025 can be returned through January 31, 2026.

More info: Returns page.

What is your Price Adjustment Policy?

If a product purchased on Vans.com or the Vans Family app drops in price within 14 days, you may be eligible for a price adjustment. See the Price Adjustment Policy for details.

How do I return my purchase to a Vans store?

We accept returns in-store for items with or without a receipt:

  • With receipt: refunded to original payment.
  • Without receipt: merchandise credit at current value.
  • Items purchased online: refunded to original card.
  • Pro Shop items (unused, original packaging & tags): return/exchange with receipt.
Can I return my Klarna order?

Returns for Klarna purchases are processed within 30 days. Continued payments may be required until the return is processed. Klarna refunds are issued within 14 days of processing.

More info: Klarna Customer Service.

Can I return my purchase made with a Vans gift card?

Returns for purchases made with a Vans gift card are processed for store credit or a new gift card, either in-store or by mail.

More info: Vans Gift Cards.
